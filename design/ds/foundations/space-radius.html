Off-ladder border, one instance

#err is the only rule that declares a tinted border: 1px solid rgba(255,93,108,.35) over --neg-dim. Everywhere else a border starts white and a state re-colours it.

State colour arrives as border-color

.lopt.right → border-color:var(--pos) + background:var(--pos-dim); .lopt.wrong → the --neg pair. #auth input:focus is the one focus border swap in the sheet: outline:none;border-color:var(--pos). Everywhere else focus is the global :focus-visible ring — outline:2px solid var(--pos), outline-offset:2px, radius 4px — never a border change. .lopt:disabled only sets cursor:default; it keeps its --hair2 edge.

The one rest → hover move between named steps

.chip:hover goes --hair → --hair2 and lifts translateY(-1px) over border-color .15s, transform .15s. The .22 / .25 hovers on .icobtn / .lopt leave the ladder entirely; .icobtn also takes color:var(--ink) and transform:rotate(30deg).

Where mobile disagrees

The React Native app does not share this system — it has a colour module only (src/theme/colors.ts) and no space or radius module at all.
No hairline on mobile

RN has no --hair equivalent. Dividers are drawn with the surface colour, opaque #262626: borderTopWidth:1, borderTopColor: colors.surfaceElevated in learn.tsx, agents.tsx, ask.tsx, settings.tsx and portfolio.tsx, and the same colour as borderBottomWidth:1 in charts.tsx (chartBox) and EquityChart.tsx. Cards themselves carry no border — only backgroundColor: colors.surface; the only bordered things are chips and quiz options, and those borders are state colours (colors.up / colors.down / colors.textMuted), never a neutral edge. So web separates with translucent white, mobile with an opaque lighter grey, and the two cannot be reconciled by a shared token today.

Different radius and surface scale

Mobile radii, counted across all screens and components: 12 ×14 (dominant — cards, risk card, position card, EmptyState), 999 ×7, 10 ×6, 2 ×3, then 4, 6 and 8 twice each and one each of 14 (learn card), 16, 18, 20, plus a 1.5 SPY dot (SPY_DOT / 2). The card radius is 12px on mobile vs 16px on web. Card padding is 16 everywhere except ask.tsx's result card (18) and EmptyState (24, with a 24 margin); the screen gutter is 24 against web's 20, and full-screen blocks like ErrorState and the portfolio hero use padding: 24 with no surface or radius at all — they are not cards. Mobile surfaces are #0a0a0a / #171717 / #262626 — three steps, all neutral grey, versus web's four blue-leaning steps.
